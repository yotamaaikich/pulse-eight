PIANO PULSE v16 pitch annotations

song-piano-data.mjs contains an independent source-harmony piano arrangement
estimated from the exact official-video audio used by each game song.
Source IDs, audio hashes, counts and analysis limits: ../../docs/song-piano-v15.json
Method, coverage and output checks: ../../docs/piano-sound-v15.md
Bass-register repairs: ../../docs/piano-register-v16.md
Per-song counts: ../../docs/song-piano-v16.json
No original song audio, lyric text or third-party MIDI/score is included.
These estimates are not a complete or exact melody transcription.

monitoring-vocal.mjs supplies the existing isolated-vocal pitch guide, which
is given priority over the full-mix harmony estimates.
Optional user-imported MML/JSON stays in this browser's localStorage.
The instrument license in CREDITS.txt covers the piano recordings, not songs.
Original music and video rights belong to their respective rights holders.
